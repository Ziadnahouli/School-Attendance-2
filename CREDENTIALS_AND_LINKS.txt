3. HOW TO CREATE NEW CUSTOM ACCOUNTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can generate unlimited custom teacher and supervisor accounts anytime:

1. Log into the Admin Dashboard:
   https://school-attendance-2-ivory.vercel.app/admin/dashboard
2. In the "Supervisors Management" card, click "+ Add Supervisor"
   - Enter email, password (min 6 chars), and assigned divisions (comma-separated).
3. In the "Teachers Management" card, click "+ Add Teacher"
   - Enter email and password (min 6 chars).
4. The new user will be immediately authorized to log in with their credentials!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW THE DAILY RESET WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Automated Schedule:
   Every night at 00:00 (Midnight), Vercel calls:
   https://school-attendance-2-ivory.vercel.app/api/cron/archive
   This moves all active absences into the historical archive.

2. On-Demand Test:
   - Click the "Archive Active Now" button inside the Admin Dashboard, OR
   - Open https://school-attendance-2-ivory.vercel.app/api/cron/archive in any browser.
